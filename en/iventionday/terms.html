2. What iVentionDay offers

The app presents stories of inventions linked to calendar dates, with a summary, a full story and images. Some entries let you open a Wikipedia article for further reading. It offers several languages, reading and appearance settings, text-to-speech and an optional daily reminder. Available translations and voices may vary; if a translation is missing, the app uses the fallback language indicated in the app.

The catalogue is included in the app and can be read offline. The app also checks ImageKit for catalogue updates and keeps a valid local copy. Images are downloaded from the catalogue's addresses, currently hosted on ImageKit, and may be cached. Updates, uncached images, ads and external links need an internet connection.

Text-to-speech uses the engine and voices installed on your device; some may need network access. Reminders depend on your enabling them, permissions and Android restrictions, so delivery may be delayed or may not occur.

The “Share” feature prepares text and an illustrated card for the external app you choose. The result depends on the destination; opening the sharing menu does not confirm that content has been sent.
