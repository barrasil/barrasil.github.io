Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Data controller: Carlos Molina Clemente.

Developer name: Barrasil Studio, the name used by the controller to publish his applications; it does not identify a separate company.

iVentionDay lets you read stories without creating an account. The catalogue is stored in the app; images and advertising use external services. This policy explains those connections and how to manage your preferences.

1. How the app works

The story catalogue is included in the app. Reading, selecting an entry and opening its details do not require registration or sending that information to a server operated by Barrasil Studio. The version described in this policy does not include a proprietary reading analytics system or Firebase Analytics.

The app and its libraries may store interface state, cached images and advertising preferences on your device. Android may manage backups of local data depending on your device and app settings.

The app does not request access to contacts, the camera, the microphone or precise location. Advertising services may still estimate an approximate location from the IP address.

2. Advertising and consent

iVentionDay integrates Google AdMob to display a banner and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices. Google's advertising SDK processes technical data: IP address, device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when available, interactions with ads and diagnostic information. These are used to serve and measure advertising, analyse the advertising service and prevent fraud.

Personalisation and the use of identifiers depend on the user's choices, service settings and applicable requirements. Before requesting ads, the app checks with UMP whether it may do so. Where applicable, it presents a form to accept, reject or manage purposes and providers. The list and the applicable legal bases for each purpose are shown in that form.

When required, the “Privacy options” (“Opciones de privacidad”) button lets you reopen your preferences. You can withdraw consent for future processing; this does not affect the lawfulness of previous processing. Rejecting personalisation does not necessarily remove all advertising or stop technical processing for permitted ads.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android's privacy settings.

3. Images, sources and external links

To display images, your device connects to the server specified in each entry. The current catalogue includes images hosted by Wikimedia, Google's patent image infrastructure and Faded Page. These servers receive the network request, including the IP address and the technical data needed to deliver the file. The app does not send names or email addresses to load images.

Opening a source, a licence or another link takes you to an external service with its own policy. You can read Wikimedia's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy; for other sites, refer to the information published by their operators.

When you use “Share”, you choose an external app that receives the invention text you decide to share. The destination is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Contact and purposes of processing

If you write to the contact email address, the controller receives your email address, your message and any data you voluntarily include, to answer your enquiry, resolve issues or handle privacy requests. Do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary. Email is managed through Gmail.

For enquiries and service maintenance, the legal basis is the legitimate interest in responding to communications and maintaining a functional app, subject to your rights. Processing that requires consent, such as certain advertising uses of identifiers or personalisation, is subject to your choice. Applicable legal obligations will also be met.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Data may be processed by Google and the advertising providers listed in the consent form, the hosts of images or pages you request, the email provider and, where appropriate, competent authorities. Each provider's role depends on its service and terms; some act as controllers for their own processing.

These services may involve processing outside the European Economic Area. Their policies and terms explain the locations, safeguards and applicable mechanisms, such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions where relevant. Google's information on data transfer frameworks details its safeguards.

6. Retention and deletion

Contact messages will be retained for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and, where appropriate, for the periods needed to comply with legal obligations or address liabilities. Data held by external services is retained according to their policies and the controls they offer; the controller does not set a single retention period for those services.

You can erase local data in Android settings or uninstall the app. This does not automatically delete data that Google or other providers have already received. To request the deletion of messages or other data you have sent directly to the controller, write to the contact email address. The app does not provide user accounts that need to be closed.

7. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability where applicable by writing to carlos.barradev@gmail.com. State your request and provide the details needed to locate the communication or processing concerned; additional identifying information will only be requested if necessary.

For data processed by third parties as controllers, also use their privacy channels. You can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your relevant supervisory authority. The AEPD explains how to exercise these rights (in Spanish).

8. This website

The Barrasil Studio website is published through GitHub Pages. Its files do not include forms, analytics, advertising or first-party cookies. The hosting provider receives technical request data, such as the IP address, to provide and protect the service. See the GitHub privacy statement.

9. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app's features, providers or data processing change. The revision date appears at the top. If a new purpose requires consent, it will be requested before that processing takes place.

For any questions about iVentionDay or this policy: carlos.barradev@gmail.com.
