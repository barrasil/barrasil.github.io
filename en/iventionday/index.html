iVentionDay brings together stories of inventions linked to each day of the calendar. Each entry includes a summary and a full story; some entries link to a Wikipedia article for further reading.

The story catalogue is included in the app and can be read offline. The app checks ImageKit for catalogue updates and downloads images from that service. Images need an internet connection when they are not cached. You can share text and illustrated cards, use text-to-speech and enable an optional daily reminder.

The app is free, requires no account and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. It includes Google AdMob advertising and Firebase Analytics usage statistics. Read the privacy policy to learn how data is handled and what choices are available.
