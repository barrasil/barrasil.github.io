iVentionDay brings together stories of inventions linked to each day of the calendar. Each entry includes a summary, a full story and links to sources for further reading.

The story catalogue is included in the app and can be read offline. Images are downloaded from their original servers and require an internet connection when they are not available in the cache.

The app includes advertising from Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy to learn how data is handled and what choices are available.
